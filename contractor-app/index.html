What the screens encode

The notification names no homeowner

Time and place only. A lock screen is read by whoever is holding the phone.

Checked by us, then how it went

Our pre-visit check and their post-visit result sit in separate sections. Blended, they read as one claim — and the first contractor to lose a bid says the appointment was never verified.

The numbers are counted, never modelled

Estimates we booked, and what the contractor told us happened. No averages, no projections, no figure we worked out on their behalf.

Territory is per tier, not per ZIP

The map says “yours at your tier” and shows who else is in the county at another one. Saying “you own this ZIP” would contradict how we actually sell.

The app never changes anything itself

Appointments, territory, service area: all of it moves through a person who confirms and tells the homeowner.

Sample data — the company, every homeowner, address, number and ZIP assignment here is invented. Prototype only: no live calendar, no live routing sheet, no real record.
