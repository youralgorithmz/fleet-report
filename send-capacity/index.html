How these numbers were read. Delivered totals come from the console's job ledger at /api/manage/send, summed across all 24 batches. Sendable-now figures are the per-list remaining counts from /api/manage/lists, which count rows uploaded to the platform and not yet sent. Workflow and list status come from /api/manage/overview. Never-texted counts are quoted from the 27 September request and were not re-measured.

Sendable-now counts what the console can enroll. Each contact is still checked against the opt-out store at the moment of sending, and against the 14-day rule, so the delivered number will always come in under the sendable one.
